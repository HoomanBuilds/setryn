# Setryn

> Risk has a date. Trade it.

We are building Setryn, a private multi-asset fixed-expiry exchange on Arbitrum One where complete future-risk positions, not isolated swaps or perpetual trades, are quoted, cleared, and managed as native instruments. Its flagship features are dated markets across FX, crypto, commodities, rates, indices, stablecoins, and tokenized assets, canonical multi-leg strategy markets, private multi-dealer RFQs and sealed auctions, direct and implied package liquidity, firm market-maker capacity reservations, portfolio clearing and netting, asset-specific benchmark and risk engines, full entry-to-settlement lifecycle automation, selective disclosure, verifiable best-execution receipts, and a professional market-maker operating system. This makes Setryn stronger than a router, treasury dashboard, or another perpetual DEX because it owns the instruments, liquidity, matching, collateral, privacy, risk, settlement, and lifecycle layer for complete dated outcomes, while existing venues expose individual trades that users must coordinate and manage themselves.

Setryn is a user-facing exchange where people, businesses, treasuries, protocols, and professional traders can lock or shape a future price, rate, index, or exchange-rate outcome without manually combining and managing positions across multiple venues.

Main features:

- Fixed-expiry markets across FX, crypto, commodities, rates, indices, stablecoins, and tokenized assets.
- Capped forwards, NDFs, options, collars, rate caps and floors, calendar spreads, basis trades, and cross-asset packages.
- Complete multi-leg strategies quoted and executed as one package.
- Standardized public markets, private RFQs, request-for-stream, sealed auctions, and batch execution.
- Competitive solvers and professional market makers with collateral-backed firm quotes.
- Portfolio clearing, scenario margin, compression, cross-position netting, and default management.
- Entry, amendment, exercise, rebalance, roll, novation, unwind, and settlement automation.
- Exposure application, strategy studio, advanced terminal, maker cockpit, risk console, SDKs, APIs, and embedded components.
- Arbitrum One mainnet execution and settlement with native USDC collateral.

Flagship features that make us unique:

- Canonical dated-risk markets where the complete future outcome is the traded instrument.
- A multi-asset protocol spanning currency, crypto, commodity, rate, index, stablecoin, and tokenized-asset risk under one clearing standard.
- Native package books with direct and implied liquidity connecting complete strategies to their component markets.
- Private multi-dealer RFQs, sealed package auctions, and selective disclosure that protect commercial and trading intent.
- Firm solver and market-maker capital reservations that make accepted quotes objectively executable.
- Formal package matching, cross-user netting, portfolio compression, and deterministic allocation.
- Asset-specific benchmark, session, rate-curve, commodity-curve, corporate-action, oracle, and disruption engines.
- Bounded payoffs, isolated risk domains, portfolio margin, default auctions, insurance, and coordinated de-risking.
- Full position lifecycle covering entry, amendment, transfer, split, merge, exercise, roll, novation, unwind, and settlement.
- Verifiable fixing, best-execution, recovery, settlement, and terminal-outcome receipts with deterministic replay.
- A market-maker operating system for quote surfaces, inventory, Greeks, collateral, hedging, limits, performance, and kill switches.
- A goal-first user experience for protecting invoices, payroll, procurement, debt, inventory, token unlocks, treasury holdings, and investment portfolios.
